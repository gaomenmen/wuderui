程
Course Structure课程结构
Phase 1: Foundations第一阶段：基础 4 weeks

Pinyin system (initials, finals, tones), basic strokes, numbers, greetings, self-introduction.拼音系统（声母、韵母、声调）、基本笔画、数字、打招呼、自我介绍。

Phase 2: Daily Life第二阶段：日常 6 weeks

Ordering food, asking directions, shopping, transportation, time & dates, making phone calls.点餐、问路、购物、交通、时间日期、打电话。

Phase 3: Fluency第三阶段：流利 8 weeks

Complex sentences, storytelling, cultural topics, travel planning, business communication basics.复杂句型、讲故事、文化话题、旅行规划、商务沟通基础。

Phase 4: Mastery第四阶段：精通 ongoing

HSK exam prep, newspaper reading, formal writing, idioms (成语), advanced conversation.HSK考试准备、报纸阅读、正式写作、成语学习、高级会话。
