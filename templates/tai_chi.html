🎋 什么是太极拳？

Tai Chi (太极拳) is a centuries-old Chinese martial art that combines gentle physical exercise with mental focus. Originating from ancient China, it's often described as "meditation in motion."

Our 24-form simplified Tai Chi course is perfect for beginners — designed by Chinese martial arts experts for international learners.
